Meet Starry & the Crashers

Starry threw the very first birthday party, and nobody came. So Starry wrote a song, built a clubhouse in the clouds, and found twelve friends who all made the same promise: no birthday alone, ever. One Crasher for every month of the year, birthstone eyes and all. Yours is down there waiting.
